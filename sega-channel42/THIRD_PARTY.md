# Dependency credits and license boundaries

- FieldStation42: use your existing installation and its license/notices. Not bundled. The source node supports executable stations; compatibility with other releases is unverified.
- RetroArch/libretro: https://github.com/libretro/RetroArch — separate project, not bundled; consult its license and the license of each core.
- Expanded-ROM core used by node1: https://github.com/BillyTimeGames/Genesis-Plus-GX-Expanded-Rom-Size — derived from Genesis Plus GX. Its recovered LICENSE.txt includes a restriction on commercial use/distribution. This package neither bundles nor relicenses that core. Obtain a compatible build from its project and read all upstream license conditions.
- Sega Channel Revival ROMs, game menus, artwork and music are separate content, not authored or licensed by this package and not included. No ROM download links are provided.

The launcher integration can be released under MIT while these independently obtained dependencies retain their own terms. Do not describe the entire emulator/game stack as MIT licensed or unrestricted open source. Sega names identify compatibility, not endorsement.
